# Van Dijk-oriented ideological interpretation v2

- Skill: `builtin.van-dijk-ideology`, version `1.7.0`.
- Prompt: `van-dijk-ideology-review-v2`.
- Output: evidence-bound interpretation of discourse, not a diagnosis of a person.

## Situate the document

Establish the document, its date and genre, institutional authorship, relevant
voices, audiences and purposes. Distinguish textual construction, contextual
facts and theoretical interpretation. External context must have identifiable
sources, dates and a declared relation to the document; later developments must
not be projected back into its production situation. A theory of context also
asks how participants make roles, purposes and common ground relevant. It does
not license claims of direct access to an individual's context model.

## Interpret ideological organization

Address identities, values, goals, norms, interests, access to resources,
institutional arrangements and social relations. Connect recurring formulations
and concrete proposals to explicitly defined analytical categories. Ideology can
be expressed without its own name or an explicit complete doctrine. When the
question asks which ideologies are manifested, offer an argued answer rather
than a topic inventory followed by blanket disclaimers.

The ideological square remains a useful comparative heuristic. It requires
justified group identification and comparable representations when used to claim
systematic asymmetry. Its complete realization is not a precondition for all
ideological interpretation. Neither a lexical item nor an external political
label alone establishes the meaning of a programme.

Distinguish fundamental ideologies from issue-specific attitudes and proposals,
personal situation/event models, communicative context models and observable
discourse. Explain how a configuration of orientations organizes policy positions
and its expression in this particular communicative situation. Configurations
vary across countries and periods and are not themselves another ideology.
In van Dijk's 2024 account, authoritarianism concerns governance, while populism
concerns a discursive strategy; other traditions may operationalize them
differently. State the adopted framework instead of treating all labels as
interchangeable ideological categories.
The left/right position of a collective is also distinct from its ideological
configuration. Descriptions of policy styles or attitudes should lead to an
explanation of their underlying ideological commitments, when warranted.

## Define the categories before testing them

These are comparative starting criteria, not mandatory labels or an exhaustive
inventory. Select categories justified by the actual document and situation.

- **Nationalism:** the nation is a privileged community of identification,
  solidarity, sovereignty and political interests. Analyse who belongs, who is
  protected and who is excluded. It need not entail ethnic purity, autarky or
  hostility to every foreigner. Those are more specific claims; their absence
  does not refute nationalism in this broader sense.
- **Economic liberalism and neoliberalism:** distinguish a general commitment to
  property and markets from a configuration prioritizing competition, private
  provision, deregulation, fiscal discipline and individual responsibility in
  organizing social relations. Explain which arrangements warrant the more
  specific category and which limit it. Welfare spending, targeted transfers or
  a strong coercive state can coexist with neoliberal arrangements. Their mere
  presence is not a counterexample; examine how they are financed, conditioned
  and related to public/private provision and access to resources.
- **Religious moral ideologies:** relate normative premises about personhood,
  life, sexuality, family, education and moral authority to sourced institutional
  traditions and public positions. A specifically Catholic genealogy can be
  argued with that context and a connected normative pattern; it does not require
  an explicit mention of the Church or a confessional self-description. Family
  support alone also does not establish Catholicism. Distinguish the movement's
  publicly documented tradition, the current document's expression and an
  individual's private religious conviction.
- **Racism and exclusionary group relations:** examine ethnic/national
  categorization, generalization, essentialization and the legitimation of
  unequal rights or resources. Racialization need not use biological vocabulary.
  Restrictive migration policies and legal-status distinctions require analysis
  of their group construction and consequences before deciding how they relate
  to racism, xenophobia or nationalism. Do not demand a literal universal slur
  as the only possible evidence or treat these categories as interchangeable.

For the discursive category of populism in this framework, identify the
construction of a virtuous people against a corrupt or self-serving elite.
Anti-ideological rhetoric, nationalism, criticism of an opponent or a general
Us/Them distinction alone do not establish that specific relation. Identify the
groups and representations rather than inferring it from a negative evaluation.

Apply the same discipline to other orientations, including antiracism, feminism
or socialist commitments where relevant: reconstruct principles, relations and
institutional proposals rather than assigning an ideology to one topic or value.

## Calibrate and test

State what supports each category and how specifically it applies. Compare
counterexamples at the same analytical level. Separate a strong general
orientation from a more specific unestablished denomination. A campaign's genre
does not make it non-ideological. A qualified interpretation is still a result;
an unsupported category should be rejected with reasons rather than by declaring
all ideological analysis impossible.

Use exact Core evidence references and disclose the actual coverage. Retrieve
more of the original immutable scope when the task needs it. Never invent
citations, context, prevalence or absence, or infer private beliefs, intentions,
psychological states or personal political affiliation from wording.

Declarations of democracy, equality or rights must be compared with the concrete
distribution of rights, resources and exclusions. They neither automatically
disprove domination nor prove hypocrisy. Implicit meanings and naturalized
assumptions can support interpretation without an explicit doctrinal confession.
Salience and omissions require coverage; a claimed historical shift requires
dated, separately inspected documents. Distinguish a documented difference from
an inferred electoral strategy or an unsupported private intention.

## Primary reference

The broader conceptual basis and discourse-level categories are discussed in
Teun A. van Dijk, *Ideology and Discourse*, especially the sections on ideological
organization and ideological discourse structures. [Author-hosted text](https://discourses.org/wp-content/uploads/2022/07/Teun-A.-van-Dijk-2003-Ideology-and-discourse.pdf).
This operationalization is a research aid; expert validation remains necessary.

Van Dijk, Teun A. (2024). *Discourse and Ideologies of the Radical Right*.
Cambridge University Press. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1017/9781009549929).
Relevant sections: 2.1 (ideologies, attitudes, models and configurations), 2.3
(terminological distinctions) and 3.1 (Chile). The author supplied the text for
this revision. Its Chilean case concerns the party's guiding principles; its
findings are not quotations or conclusions established about the 2025 programme.
