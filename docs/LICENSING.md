# Licensing

## Public DiscourseLens license

DiscourseLens-authored software in this repository is licensed under the GNU
Affero General Public License, version 3 only (AGPL-3.0-only); see the root
[LICENSE](../LICENSE).

The AGPL allows commercial use. A person or company may use, change, sell, or
provide the software as a service without paying DiscourseLens, provided they
comply with the AGPL. When distributing object code, the license requires
corresponding source to be made available under its terms. Section 13 also
requires an offer of corresponding source to users interacting with a modified
version over a network.

## Separate commercial license for DiscourseLens

The copyright holders may grant a separate commercial license for the
DiscourseLens-authored code to organizations that need proprietary terms or do
not want to comply with the AGPL's source-sharing requirements. That agreement
would cover only rights the signing holders control. It would not revoke the
AGPL rights already granted to other recipients, and it would not cover
third-party dependencies.

No price list, signed commercial agreement, or dedicated licensing contact is
currently configured in this repository. Before accepting payment, the project
must publish the commercial terms and a private contact channel, and confirm
that the relevant rights holders can grant the promised rights. Contributions
remain under AGPL unless their authors separately grant commercial relicensing
rights in writing.

## PyMuPDF is licensed separately

PyMuPDF is a required dependency. Its maintainers offer it under the GNU AGPL v3
or a separate commercial license from Artifex. A DiscourseLens commercial
license does not include Artifex rights, and an Artifex license does not include
rights to DiscourseLens. A customer choosing proprietary terms must arrange the
necessary Artifex license directly with Artifex, unless a later written
distribution or reseller agreement says otherwise.

The project currently uses the AGPL route for the public source release. Before
publishing a prebuilt installer or container under a commercial distribution
model, confirm with Artifex that the specific delivery model and any bundled
PyMuPDF components are covered. Artifex describes its commercial agreements as
case-specific and offers SaaS, OEM, and subscription models; see its
[licensing page](https://artifex.com/licensing).

## What this model does not promise

This model cannot require every commercial user to pay. Users may choose the
public AGPL license and conduct commercial activity while meeting its terms.
The separate paid DiscourseLens license is an option for proprietary terms, not
a mandatory fee for all profit-making use. Requiring payment for all commercial
use would require a different license arrangement for the integrated program;
do not add a noncommercial-only license to this repository while PyMuPDF remains
an integrated AGPL dependency without first resolving that compatibility with
qualified counsel and Artifex.
